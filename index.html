Contra accounts reduce another account, so they behave the opposite way to it.

  • Accounts Payable: amounts still owed to suppliers or employees. Where a specific liability account exists (e.g. Warranty Liability, Dividends Payable), use that account instead.
  • Accounts Receivable: amounts owed to the company by customers and others.
  • Interest Payable / Interest Receivable: interest owed or earned but not yet paid. Accounts Payable / Accounts Receivable are also accepted for interest.
  • Loans Payable: bank loans. Cash includes bank accounts.
